A call, not a coin flip. Every posted price is stripped of its margin and measured against what the rest of the market says about the same player, with each side's expected goals read straight off the result market. Where they disagree, there is an edge. Where they don't, we say PASS.

LevelThree-way result price

Each side’s expected goals come from its fixture’s FanDuel 1X2 on the live-lines page, de-vigged and solved for both Poisson means — Liverpool 1.10 v Arsenal 1.31.

PriceDraftKings only

The export names the book with the best price. 13 rows are DraftKings-best and show that price. 29 rows are best at a book ShortOpen does not carry; the export does not say which of their other prices is DraftKings’, so they show no price and only the market’s own read — the de-vigged median of every price on the player.

ModelThe other books

A DraftKings price is never graded against itself: the reference is the other books’ median, de-vigged and scaled to how that price column usually runs against the rest, so a book that is simply always longer is not called an edge.

Grading0 strong · 0 lean

STRONG at +2% EV or better on three prices, LEAN at +0.5% on two, with the books’ own disagreement moved against the call. A player listed doubtful is shown and not graded.

What is not hereHonest accounting

Players with one price (every assist row) are not carried, per the two-book rule. Keeper saves are not carried: every keeper on a club, backups included, is posted at the same price, so the export cannot say who starts.
