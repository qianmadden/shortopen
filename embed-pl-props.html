LevelThree-way result price

Each side’s expected goals come from its Matchweek 10 FanDuel 1X2 (captured Nov 4), de-vigged and solved for both Poisson means — Arsenal 2.83 against Hull the highest, Hull 0.55 the lowest.

ModelThe player’s share

Each player’s share of his team’s goals is backed out of the Matchweek 9 market read — the books’ prices, de-vigged, against that week’s team xG — and applied to the Matchweek 10 xG. Clean sheets are e−xG of the opponent.

Fair priceNot yet

There is no Matchweek 10 price to de-vig, so fair price, break-even, edge and EV show a dash. The model’s own fair odds are shown so a posted price can be compared by eye.

GradingWaits for prices

When prices post, a DraftKings price is graded against the other books on the same player, never against itself: STRONG at +2% EV on three prices, LEAN at +0.5% on two.
