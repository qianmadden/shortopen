How The Call Is MadeThree inputs, each doing a job the other two cannot. The market sets the level, the book sets the shape, and the opponent's actual defensive record is the only part the bookmaker does not control.
Level1X2 and the posted total

Two quotes set the level and they do different jobs. The three-way price fixes the ratio of the two sides' scoring rates — it is three numbers about who wins. The over/under quotes their sum, which is what a clean sheet actually turns on. Both are de-vigged and the pair pins each side's expected goals, replacing the flat 1.064 factor carried since Matchweek 5; measured per fixture that factor really runs 0.886 to 1.194. Brentford v Liverpool: the 1X2 splits it 1.65 to 1.48 and the posted over/under sizes it at 3.13 goals, and every expected-goals number on this card is built the same way.

ShapeGoalscorer market

The ordering of a team's attackers encodes role, minutes and set-piece duty — the things bookmakers genuinely price well. We take that ordering and nothing else from it, then re-spread the team's expected output across the top five by those weights.

Independent readDefensive record

Goals and shots on target conceded per 90 by the opponent so far this season. This is the one input the book does not set, so it is where disagreement — and therefore edge — comes from. Five matches is a small sample and the table carries no attack information, so it is shrunk to 30% of the blend rather than trusted whole.

Clean sheetsTwo-way, exact

The only genuinely two-sided market of the three, so its margin comes off exactly rather than by assumption — roughly 8% hold instead of the goalscorer market's inflation. It is also the input that sets every expected-goals number above, and it is graded here against the keeping side's own defensive record, which is not what set the price.

Why edge, not EVHonest accounting

One-sided goalscorer markets are heavily juiced. Measured against raw break-even every selection looks unbettable, which hides the signal. So the call is graded on model versus the margin-stripped fair price, and the true EV at the listed price is shown beside it so nothing is flattered.
