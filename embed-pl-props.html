How The Call Is MadeMatchweek 10 anytime-goalscorer prices from DraftKings, bet365 and SBK (via oddsindex, Nov 6), all ten fixtures. Each card shows the best price on the player, named, graded against the other books on the same player.
LevelThree-way result price

Each side’s expected goals come from its Matchweek 10 FanDuel 1X2, de-vigged and solved for both Poisson means — Arsenal 2.85 against Hull at the top, Hull 0.52 at the bottom.

PriceBest of the books

Every row has at least two prices. The best one is shown with its book: DraftKings on 217 rows, SBK on 197. bet365 is in the comparison wherever it posts.

ModelThe other books

A price is never graded against itself: the reference is the other books’ median, de-vigged and scaled to how the best price’s book usually runs against them, so a book that is simply always longer is not called an edge.

Grading16 strong · 14 lean

STRONG at +2% EV or better on three prices, LEAN at +0.5% on two, with the books’ own disagreement moved against the call. A price far out of line with the rest is shown but not graded — that is usually team news.

What is not hereHonest accounting

Players with one price are not carried — that includes every assist row this week. Keeper saves are not carried: every keeper on a club, backups included, is posted at the same price, so the prices cannot say who starts. Prediction-market reads (Polymarket, Kalshi) are noted on a card where they exist, never used as the price.
