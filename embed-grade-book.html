How This Board Is Built
Every player starts from a synthesized read across 12 industry grading sources — not a single outlet's number. Short Open converts that consensus into a letter grade (A+ to C+), then layers three things on top: a Trend read pulled from the Movers Desk (Ascending, Volatile or Steady), a role/usage lens specific to the position, and a betting translation that explains what the grade does and doesn't tell you about a market. The board is a static snapshot refreshed each cycle, not a live feed — the hero status bar always shows which season's board is loaded.
Grade Book Archive
This cycle's board carries 10 tracked risers and 10 tracked fallers in the Movers Desk, spanning every level of the depth chart — from Sam Darnold's Super Bowl-run climb to Jayden Daniels' injury-shortened drop. Historical grade movement compounds: a player who shows up in consecutive cycles' riser or faller lists is flagged first in the Deep Sleepers and Profiles to Monitor modules, since sustained movement is a stronger signal than any single-season jump.
