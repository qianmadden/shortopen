How probabilities are built

Every American price on this board comes from our own model after aggregating live market data across books, and is converted with the standard no-stake implied-probability formula: for a favorite (negative odds) it's |odds| / (|odds| + 100); for an underdog (positive odds) it's 100 / (odds + 100). That per-leg number is what "win%" and the bar chart show — it is a price-implied probability, not a guaranteed outcome, and it still carries the book's built-in vig.

When you simulate a ticket, each selected leg becomes an independent weighted coin. The simulator flips all of them together for N draws (your chosen draw count) and counts how often every single leg landed in the same draw. That hit rate — not a stake, not a payout — is what's shown as P(all legs). Combined price is shown separately, for reference only, as the product of each leg's decimal odds.

Aggregated, not invented

Every price on this board is aggregated from multiple sportsbooks to build a general consensus, then run through our own model — no invented lines, no made-up odds.

Probability, not payout

This tool never shows a stake, a potential return, or a dollar payout. Every number on this page is a probability — implied from the price, or simulated from it.

Research preview

Independent-leg simulation is a baseline. Same-game correlation, injury news and closing-line movement can all shift the real probability — this is a starting point, not a promise.
