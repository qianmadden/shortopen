Source layer

The 2026 Week 1 schedule and current depth-chart structure set the player pool. Defensive profiles use the supplied 2026 position-group rankings for defensive line, edge, linebacker, cornerback and safety. Publicly unavailable placements are marked (est.) and carry lower confidence. No single-player panel should be read as a one-on-one assignment.
