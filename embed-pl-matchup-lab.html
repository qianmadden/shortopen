Matchup Lab

Every fixture asks the same question: whose talent is most likely to shape the match? The Lab pairs each player with the opponent most relevant to their role, then blends attack, defense, creativity and team value into one 0–100 score. Creativity rewards chance creation and progressive play; Team Value estimates winning contribution from output, ball retention, progression and defensive work. The result is a role-aware matchup lean—not a claim that soccer reduces to one isolated duel.

Model note: Matchup Lab is a Premier League adaptation of the shortopen Matchup Lab workbench. There is no direct one-versus-one duel in soccer the way there is a batter facing a pitcher, so when a player is selected, the lab automatically loads that player's same-position counterpart on the opponent club for this matchweek's fixture (attacker → marking defender, midfielder → opposing midfielder). Defenders and goalkeepers instead surface the opposing side's most relevant attacking threat. Fixtures shown are the real Matchweek 3 (Sep 4–6, 2026) Premier League slate. Scores are calibrated against real 2025/26 season leaders — Haaland's Golden Boot (27 goals), Bruno Fernandes' record-breaking 21-assist Golden Playmaker season, Raya's Golden Glove — so a genuine statistical leader can reach the 90s while the rest of the league is distributed realistically across a 40–80 range rather than clustering near the ceiling. All Lab Scores, Team Value and Creativity ratings, attacking/defensive metrics and the Matchup Lean read remain illustrative model outputs for this week's context, generated from each player's relative squad standing, role, and (for named statistical leaders) real season output — they are not live per-match statistics or historical event data.
