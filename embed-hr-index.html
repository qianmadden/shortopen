Bat-Power Model

What the Home Run Index measures

The HR Index is a pure bat-power model. It blends quality of contact (Barrel%, Hard Hit%, EV90), expected power (xSLG), raw pop (ISO) and hitting-or-missing (K%) into a single Bat Impact score, then projects a ballpark-adjusted HR Chance/PA and compounds it across a game's plate appearances into HR Model/Game. It says nothing about defense, baserunning, or total value — only how likely a hitter is to leave the yard.

HR Model/Game = 1 − (1 − HR Chance/PA)^(PA per Game)
